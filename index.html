Bringing Las Vegas to
Kwara State

Every year, Amazon Web Services holds its flagship global cloud conference, AWS re:Invent, in Las Vegas, Nevada. Tens of thousands of engineers, creators, and tech pioneers gather to witness ground-breaking unveilings in generative AI, high-performance cloud infrastructure, serverless architectures, and next-gen developer tools.

This year, you do not need an international flight to feel that electric energy. The AWS Student Builder Group (SBG) University of Ilorin and AWS Student Builder Group (SBG) Kwara State University (KWASU) have unified forces to bring that frontier innovation straight home to Kwara State.

Hosted at the world-class Ilorin Innovation Hub, this free, hands-on collaborative event dissolves institutional boundaries. Whether you are an undergraduate building your first full-stack application, an aspiring cloud engineer preparing for certifications, or a passionate local developer, you belong in the room.

Campus Transit Directions

For UNILORIN Students (PS & Tanke):

Take a cab or tricycle from University Permanent Site (PS) or Tanke / Tipper Garage towards Fate / GRA axis. The Innovation Hub is centrally located and easily accessible via commercial transit or ride-hailing (Bolt/inDrive).

For KWASU Students (Malete):

Board the regular Malete-to-Ilorin student shuttle or commercial cab to Post Office / Maraba. From Post Office, connect via a 10-minute drop or tricycle directly to Ilorin Innovation Hub. Student carpooling and convoy coordination will also be arranged in our WhatsApp community!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Is the AWS re:Invent Watch & Build Party free to attend? ▼

Yes, 100% free! Admission, access to keynote streams, guided hands-on build mentorship, community swag, and light refreshments are completely free. However, space is strictly capped at venue capacity, so RSVPing on Luma is mandatory.

Where do I register for the event? ▼

Official registration and attendee check-in happens directly through Luma. While our student community groups stay active year-round on WhatsApp and Meetup, event access and seating passes are managed on Luma.

I am a complete beginner to AWS. Can I still attend? ▼

Absolutely! AWS Student Builder Groups are designed specifically to welcome and empower novices. We have technical floor leads from UNILORIN and KWASU ready to guide you step-by-step through setting up your first cloud service.

Can non-students or developers from outside UNILORIN and KWASU attend? ▼

Yes! While organized collaboratively by the student builder groups of UNILORIN and KWASU, the event is open to tech enthusiasts, bootcamp students, self-taught coders, and professionals across the Kwara State tech ecosystem.

Do I need a paid AWS account? ▼

No! You can use an AWS Free Tier account or an AWS Builder ID. All workshop exercises are architected strictly within the AWS Free Tier boundaries so no credit charges occur.
