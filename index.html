1. Laptop & Charger Mandatory
Tip
Bring your laptop with sufficient battery life and its power adapter. macOS, Linux, and Windows are all fully supported for our hands-on workshop labs.
2. Extension Multi-Plug Recommended
Tip
Power outlets are abundant across the Ilorin Innovation Hub hall, but having a compact multi-plug or surge protector guarantees convenient desk power access for you and your seat neighbors.
3. Active AWS Builder ID / Account Essential
Tip
Set up a free AWS Builder ID or AWS Free Tier Account before arriving to bypass SMS or card verification queues during the lab.
4. VS Code Pre-installed Ready Setup
Tip
Have Visual Studio Code installed. Recommended extensions: AWS Toolkit for VS Code and Git.

Tip
Bring your questions and collaborative spirit! Student mentors from UNILORIN and KWASU will guide you through setting up real AWS architectures regardless of your prior experience.

Frequently Asked Questions · Knowledge Base

Everything You Need to Know

Is the AWS re:Invent Watch & Build Party free to attend? ▼

Yes, 100% free! Admission, access to keynote streams, guided hands-on build mentorship, community swag, and light refreshments are completely free. However, space is strictly capped at venue capacity, so claiming your pass on Trankets or AWS Builder Center is required.

Where do I register for the event? ▼

Official registration and attendee check-in happens primarily through Trankets (trankets.com/events/aws-re-invent), our main ticketing platform. You can also claim your pass via the official AWS Builder Center (builder.aws.com). Both portals issue official event admission passes.

I am a complete beginner to AWS. Can I still attend? ▼

Absolutely! AWS Student Builder Groups are designed specifically to welcome and empower novices. We have technical floor leads from UNILORIN and KWASU ready to guide you step-by-step through setting up your first cloud service.

Can non-students or developers from outside UNILORIN and KWASU attend? ▼

Yes! While organized collaboratively by the student builder groups of UNILORIN and KWASU, the event is open to tech enthusiasts, bootcamp students, self-taught coders, and professionals across the Kwara State tech ecosystem.

Do I need a paid AWS account? ▼

No! You can use an AWS Free Tier account or an AWS Builder ID. All workshop exercises are architected strictly within the AWS Free Tier boundaries so no credit charges occur.

Where can I follow official event updates, speaker drops, and build challenges? ▼

Follow the official event accounts on X (@AWSRE_INVENT), Instagram (@awsre.invent), TikTok (@awsreinvent), and LinkedIn (AWS re:Invent Watch & Build)! We post keynote alerts, speaker profiles, event date confirmations, and live streams across these pages.
